# Tournaments

UI/UX Pro Max's sports tournament and event registration search recommended a photo-led hero, condensed display type, and one primary action. CueLab uses its own 10-ball trophy photo and the venue palette from the master design system.

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page and reference field | `#FBF7F3` | `#191418` |
| Event rows and lookup panel | `#FFFDFB` | `#271E24` |
| Primary text | `#211A1B` | `#FFF3EE` |

| Primary action fill / text | `#A92032` / `#FFF8F5` | `#A92032` / `#FFF8F5` |

Secondary text measures at least 6.76:1 across both themes and designated surfaces. Date text measures 5.81:1 in light mode and 7.22:1 in dark mode. The reference field border exceeds 3:1 against both the field fill and lookup panel in each theme. Primary action text measures 6.80:1 against its fill.

Use Barlow Condensed 600 at 48–80 px for the page title and 28–40 px for section headings. Use Geist for body text, schedule details, and controls. Give the photo and panels 16 px radii, and controls 8 px radii with a minimum 44 px target.

Lead with the page title, a short explanation, and a link to the schedule. When no tournaments are listed, link to the registration lookup instead. Present the photo beside the title on wider screens and after the intro on phones.

List events in schedule order with a date tile, start time, fee, and link to the existing event page. Show the schedule and reference lookup side by side when space allows, and stack them in that order on smaller screens. Keep the lookup available when the schedule is empty. Associate lookup errors with the reference field and provide a visible 2 px focus outline.
