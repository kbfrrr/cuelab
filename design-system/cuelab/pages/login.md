# Staff sign-in

Apply the CueLab system to `/admin/login`. Center a single form panel up to 448 px wide with 16 px outer gutters, 24 px inner padding on phones, and 32 px inner padding from 640 px. Keep the layout scrollable on short screens.

Center the CueLab logo and the **Staff sign in** heading above the form. Left-align the email and password labels and **Remember me**. Follow those with the full-width **Sign in** button and a **Back to CueLab** link. Use a 32–40 px display heading and 48 px minimum heights for the fields and submit button.

Use the existing Filament form and authentication behavior. Keep the password visibility control, autocomplete, paste, field errors, and submit feedback available. The primary action is sign-in. The home link returns staff to the guest landing page.
