# Contact page

UI/UX Pro Max’s sports venue search informed the condensed heading, red action, and single point of emphasis. Use the existing CueLab light and dark tokens for the page, map frame, details panel, and links. Pair Barlow Condensed 600 at 40–64 px with Geist for address and contact details. The ivory text on the cranberry action measures 6.8:1.

Start with **Visit CueLab**, the verified address, and **Get directions**. Place the linked map to the right from 768 px and below the directions on smaller screens. Follow with phone and social details in a single panel. Use the existing venue-name Maps search when the address is pending. Publish contact links from verified settings, with a callable phone number and a valid HTTPS social URL.

Keep the page focused on visiting and contacting the venue. Use visible 2 px focus outlines, an 8 px action radius, and a 16 px radius for the map and details.
